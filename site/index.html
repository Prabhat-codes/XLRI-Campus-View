About this map

A 3D model of the XLRI Jamshedpur campus and roughly 5 km around it, built from open data.

  • Buildings: footprints from Overture Maps (Google Open Buildings, Microsoft ML Buildings and OpenStreetMap). Heights are estimates, since none of the sources record them here.
  • Names, roads, water: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
  • Ground imagery: Esri World Imagery (Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics).
  • Trees: placed wherever the satellite image shows canopy; individual trees are not surveyed.
  • Neighbourhood labels: positioned approximately.

An unofficial fan project, not affiliated with XLRI.
